Terrain sources

Ironfront combines and transforms real-world elevation samples into fictional geography. Samples are resampled, rotated, reflected, warped, blended, and eroded; generated maps are not geographic representations of real places.

Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed September 9, 2026. The bundled sample manifest records source tile URLs, SHA-256 checksums, bounds, and processing resolution. Dataset attribution and licenses.
