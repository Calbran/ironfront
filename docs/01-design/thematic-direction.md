# Theme and Factions

**Accepted aesthetic:** Cold War era steampunk blended with Victorian influence. Simple unit representations.

Cold War silhouettes, Victorian identity, steampunk engineering. Radar, armored formations, turbines, command bunkers, riveted steel, pressure systems, imperial uniforms, and industrial cities. Machinery should read by function before ornament. No requirement for 3D or individual animated soldiers.

## Provisional faction roster

| Faction | Character | Full-game direction | Proof bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Directorate | Central industrial state; red markings, heavy steel | Deliberate armored advances; heavy landship | +10% attack power |
| Crownward League | Victorian institutions with modern radar; blue, ivory, brass | Defense and recovery; armored guards | +15% defense power |
| Aether Compact | Experimental federation; teal, copper, cream | Mobile war and air support; turbine gunship | Air support uses 1 fuel per combat resolution instead of 2 |

Names, ideology, bonuses, and signature units are working concepts, not locked canon. Faction and nation are different: several players may choose the same faction. Player colors indicate ownership; faction symbols indicate identity. No mandatory login pattern is attached to a faction.

## First presentation

An operational map dominates the screen. Muted sea and land, clearly different ownership colors, crisp region boundaries, small rectangular army counters, selected-region inspector, and a field dispatch log. Use simple vector terrain symbols. No concept illustrations have been generated; do not describe interface geometry as concept art.
