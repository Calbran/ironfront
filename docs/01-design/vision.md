# Vision

**Status: accepted direction, September 9, 2026.** Exact numbers are provisional.

A month-long war where a few minutes of planning shape the next day. The player opens a browser, sees where front lines shifted, understands why, makes a handful of plans, and returns to ordinary life. Friends and strangers share the same persistent campaign.

## Core promises

- Conquest and land ownership are the goal; buildings support that goal.
- A generated continent and player-scaled regions make each campaign different.
- Standing orders continue without a connected browser.
- Basic national development, infantry, artillery, land vehicles, and aircraft provide readable choices.
- Factions offer recognizable identity and modest asymmetry.
- Cold War structure, Victorian identity, and steampunk engineering establish the world.

## Session loop

Read what changed → inspect a front → choose an army objective → queue development → leave. A five-minute session should be useful. Eight hours away should produce understandable consequences rather than punishment for being absent.

## What this is not trying to prove yet

No detailed tactical battle control, naval war, sprawling research tree, economic market, hero collection, or weighted victory zones. Different victory modes can be designed separately later.

## Success measures

Playtest whether a new player can explain the goal, issue useful orders without coaching, understand a defeat from the report, and leave confidently. Track actions/session and unnecessary check-ins before expanding complexity. No engagement or balance results exist yet.
