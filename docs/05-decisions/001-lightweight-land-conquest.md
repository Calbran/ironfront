# Decision 001: Lightweight land conquest

**Status:** accepted direction, 2026-09-09. Numerical rules are provisional.

## Context

The owner wants a browser game that fits ordinary life, with a month-long generated-continent war and simple nation management. Victory centered on valuable zones was explicitly rejected for the default mode.

## Decision

Land owned determines the default winner. Persistent standing orders and readable front-line changes anchor short sessions. Include approachable faction identity and a Cold War/Victorian/steampunk visual setting. Friends and strangers are both eventual audiences.

## Consequences

Economically valuable regions can support war but never earn extra victory points. Area scoring, 28-day duration, >50% for 48 hours, and tie handling are concrete proof hypotheses. They remain subject to owner/playtest revision. Key-zone modes are separate future work.
