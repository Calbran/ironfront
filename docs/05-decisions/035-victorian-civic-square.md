# 035 — Victorian civic square

## Accepted direction

The user wants less empty, bare city space and specifically a town hall framed by low shrub/stone walls, with stronger Victorian/steampunk character.

## Implemented in the diorama

The existing hall reservation contains capped stone walls with front and side openings, clipped shrubs in planting beds and benches. The owner subsequently requested the whole street-bounded block: four front shops now occupy a connected rear market row instead. The walls and broader planting beds reach the street verges, with a central approach and gated openings. Exact building counts remain unchanged. The hall gains a pediment, dormers, copper rooftop vessels/pipes and clock trim at its existing scale. Static details share material batches; extruded and indexed geometries normalize before merging.

## Limits

This is one authored civic composition. Automatic civic parcels, generic garden fitting, functional doors, collision/cover and capital capture remain separate future work. Daylight glass is uniform; dusk uses stable off/dim/bright window states. Broader block variety remains on the city roadmap.
