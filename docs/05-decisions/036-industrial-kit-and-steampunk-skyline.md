# 036 — Industrial kit and steampunk skyline

## Accepted direction

The user authorized mills/service-yard work and supplied Victorian/steampunk skyline references, asking for taller skyscrapers and simplified accents. Use domes, steep roof crowns, vertical masonry, copper plant/pipes and iron details at miniature scale. Reference imagery informs motifs, not a requirement to reproduce its buildings, rail systems or density.

## Implemented in the study

Wide mills have multiple roof lights, tall banded stacks and external plant. Boiler houses include copper boilers and coal-storage bays. Nine-storey commercial towers have vertical facade bands, setback crowns, domes and spires. Floor/window dimensions remain comparable to ordinary buildings; height comes from additional floors rather than vertical stretching.

Industrial blocks now have two wider plots per frontage. Selected commercial frontages reserve two adjacent slots for one tower. Wider model dimensions feed the existing overlap/street checks, and exact requested building counts remain. Existing window-state shader and at most 12 smoke sources are reused. Industry and Skyline presets support review.

## Provisional and remaining

Placement frequency, heights and proportions are art tuning. This is the first tower family, not a complete skyscraper catalog. Real facade/foundation/attachment envelopes, service-yard planning beyond these assets, LODs and varied block shapes remain in the roadmap. No recruitable airships, trains, traversable skybridges, production/capture rules or campaign rollout are implied. Generated assets remain shared Three.js geometry, not imported reference art.
