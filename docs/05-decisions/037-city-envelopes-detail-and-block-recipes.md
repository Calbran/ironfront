# 037 — City envelopes, detail levels and block recipes

## Accepted direction

The user authorized building bounds, distant-view simplification, then block variety while preserving unit/building scale and the civic square.

## Implemented in the diorama

Study lots opt into conservative centered horizontal envelopes for roofs, awnings, steps, scaffolds and pipes. Mesh-bound browser validation checks the contract. Legacy organic/world plans retain their prior spacing behavior. Side-shop spacing and rear market alignment accommodate full awnings.

Shared geometry is built into full and distant kits. The distant tier retains major volumes, roofs, domes, stacks and snow, dropping small primitives such as window panes, trim and rails. Projected world-unit size selects the tier with 3.5/4.5-pixel hysteresis. It is a whole-study detail switch, not district culling. Geometry/material ownership includes both tiers and shared snow pieces.

Residential recipes vary between courtyard wings and stepped frontages; commercial and industrial zoning remain. The seed changes actual positions/orientations through a Vary blocks button. Counts stay exact, scale stays fixed and the civic block remains reserved.

## Limits

Envelopes are conservative horizontal rectangles, not authoritative 3D collision or walkable entrances. Streets retain the existing rectangular framework. Courtyards are developed paved space, not simulated amenities. Terrain-aware irregular blocks, partial polygons, district culling and campaign rollout are unfinished. Detail thresholds and recipes remain provisional art/performance tuning. Small window lights are omitted in the distant tier; no additional lights are added.

## Composition follow-up — 2026-09-10

The user chose block composition before expanding the model library. The study now records each district lot's front entrance and its outward street connection. Handed corner shops glaze only the exposed end wall. A third residential recipe uses shallow terraces with rear gardens and a central service lane; courtyard and stepped recipes remain. Vacant frontage pockets receive bounded gardens where building and garden reservations permit. These are decorative access paths, not campaign navigation. Street framework and garden dimensions remain provisional; arbitrary parcels and terrain fitting are still future work.
