# 039 — Terrain fitting study

## Accepted direction

The user authorized fitting the angled district to slopes and riverbanks while retaining its frontages and courtyards.

## Implemented

A selectable Fit to hills & river comparison raises the northern district by six miniature units along a smooth graded profile. Streets and subdivided parcel surfaces sample that profile. Buildings retain vertical orientation and architectural scale; level foundation caps use the highest footprint sample, with skirts meeting the local terrain and stepped front approaches. Candidates with more than 1.2 units of footprint relief or 1.1 units of entrance rise are omitted. The fitted count is displayed.

A protected northern channel sits outside the parcels. A road extends from the existing northwest junction across it on a raised deck with parapets, reaching the opposite bank. Ground carving is separate from road grade and water elevation. The flat angled study and original city remain selectable. Tests cover deterministic fits, dry footprints, foundation height, new-road grade bounds and steep-site rejection.

## Limits and provisional tuning

This is a one-dimensional authored elevation profile on a fixed street network, not general terrain-first street routing. The northern river is deliberately outside the existing parcels; arbitrary river cuts, drainage, 2D hills, connected terracing and alternate road routing on steep sites are unfinished. No navigation, campaign authority or capture changes. Foundation limits, grades and stair dimensions are provisional art tuning. The profile keeps northern streets below 11% grade; this does not validate gameplay travel or accessibility.
