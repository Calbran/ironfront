# 045 — District infill and fitted foundations

## Accepted direction

Use empty areas deliberately, expand commercial/industrial blocks, connect district surfaces and remove foundations that do not match their buildings.

## Shipped behavior

Combined frontage fitting tries the original building, small inward setbacks and compatible smaller models before rejecting a site. Entrances follow the fitted model. Parcel, courtyard, bank, road, terrain and overlap checks remain active. Suitable residual space receives clear cargo yards/tanks or seating with planters; access paths remain unobstructed. A paved forecourt spans the civic-to-district transition.

Foundations use actual building footprints, the same rigid pose as the model and inverse terrain heights for their skirts. They no longer use clearance envelopes or get deformed with the river. Brick skirts sit beneath the existing model plinth.

## Provisional tuning

Setback attempts, prop budgets and the forecourt dimensions are authored study tuning. Space may remain open where safe placement fails; no unsafe building is forced into a lot. General interior service-road subdivision and arbitrary civic placement remain future work.
