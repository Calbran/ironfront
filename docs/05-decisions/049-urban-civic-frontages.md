# 049 — Urban civic frontages and street utilities

## Accepted direction

The full-city town hall should have urban frontage blocks around it rather than the original canopy shops and empty town-template land. Bridge decks should visually continue the roads. Add restrained steampunk street utilities and steam-emitting manholes.

## Implemented in the visual study

Full-city mode starts with no authored home/shop lots. Three fitted commercial frontage parcels surround the actual hall square (street boundaries x ±24, z -18/19), replacing the previous oversized civic reserve. Their building envelopes, entrances and foundations pass the same fitter as other city blocks. The central hall and garden paths remain protected.

Arched bridges retain structural sides and railings but use the carriageway material and center markings on their decks. Ordinary bridge ribbons use road surfacing too.

The kit adds instanced manhole covers, valve stands and vent cabinets. Up to 24 covers are sampled away from junctions, water and parked cars. Up to eight emit short, camera-facing steam plumes in the existing particle batch. Street utility props reuse the clearance-checked accent placement and distant-detail system. These visuals do not add tactical obstacles or gameplay mechanics.

## Validation and limits

Civic-clearance tests now protect the actual square and prohibit the old home/shop variants in full-city plans. The existing seed audits still check connected roads, building overlaps, entrances, water clearance and foundation relief. Art density and steam settings are provisional; the new geometry is not authoritative combat cover.
