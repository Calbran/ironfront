# Bounded local squad support

Accepted 2026-09-11: squads should support nearby fighting against visible enemies and react together while players are offline, while respecting explicit player orders.

One shared game-core policy serves city and country adapters. Autonomy is local and bounded; it does not grant omniscient information, override Hold, or initiate long-range pursuit. Initial distances and cadence are provisional tuning. Country scenario rosters are shared by authority and presentation.

See [local squad support](../02-systems/local-squad-support.md) for behavior, validation boundaries and remaining work.
