# Real map settlement layouts

Status: accepted direction to improve settlement structure with real-world samples; implementation and art tuning remain provisional.

The owner rejected repeated gridded modules. Use actual street networks and clusters as reviewable references, and grow procedural sites along roads with varied density. Keep individual buildings at city-kit scale. Extending a settlement must not merely enlarge meshes or repeat identical blocks.

The first adapter uses small offline OpenStreetMap/Overpass extracts. Preserve source provenance and ODbL attribution; make the filtered data downloadable. Street geometry guides placement while clearance checks may omit or locally move substituted models. Do not claim exact reconstruction or combat integration. Keep original art assets and map-derived data clearly identified.

Current scope and limits: [country library](../02-systems/country-set-pieces.md).
