# Ironfront — First art asset brief

Working proposal · September 9, 2026

Goal: original Victorian industrial strategy-game art with blackened iron, aged brass and military enamel. Material character should resemble functional equipment. Use the supplied images for general direction only; do not reproduce their artwork, watermarks or insignia.

## 1. HUD concept sheet

Prompt:

> Create an original UI art concept sheet for Ironfront, a Victorian industrial steampunk strategy game. Blackened iron structures, aged brass bevels, dark green enamel button faces, restrained screws and corner fittings, believable shallow material depth, consistent upper-left lighting. Include a wide top resource-bar frame, a bottom unit-command tray with a portrait recess, small rectangular command buttons in raised/pressed/disabled states, an inset horizontal supply-meter housing, and a circular minimap bezel. All reading areas are clean, quiet and empty. No text, numbers, logos, watermarks, portraits or scenery. Avoid excessive gears, fantasy ornament and bright gold. Arrange pieces with generous separation on a plain neutral background. This is a cohesive concept sheet, not a finished interactive screen.

After direction is chosen: request separate transparent pieces, including corners and repeating edges. Keep scalable empty centers; verify button-state dimensions match. Do not stretch the whole concept sheet into a HUD.

## 2. Unit-card triptych

Prompt:

> Create three original, equally sized strategy-game unit illustrations in one coherent Victorian industrial world: a Crownward rifle squad in navy wool uniforms with practical brass fittings; an Iron Directorate riveted heavy landship with charcoal armor and dark red enamel markings; an Aether Compact copper-and-teal turbine interceptor. Grounded military engineering with restrained steampunk detail, atmospheric painterly realism, clear silhouettes, consistent upper-left lighting and subdued backgrounds. Three separate equal panels, no text, borders, faction logos or historical symbols. Each subject remains legible when reduced to a small unit card. Do not imitate named game characters or copy supplied reference assets.

After approval: generate each card separately in the same framing and palette, allowing safe crop margins.

## 3. Terrain/building study

Prompt:

> Create an original asset-direction sheet for a Victorian industrial strategy map, consistent near-overhead orthographic view with shallow visible building sides. Show separate groups: a rural hamlet, compact town street blocks, a smoky factory district, an iron-and-brick supply depot, and clustered temperate forest canopy. Muted natural land colors, slate roofs, weathered brick, restrained brass details, common upper-left sunlight and short consistent shadows. Clear silhouettes readable from high altitude. No labels, UI, characters, ownership colors, watermarks or dramatic perspective. Separate groups with empty space; do not connect them into a single fixed map.

After approval: extract or regenerate isolated transparent variants at a common scale. Use code-generated terrain and ownership beneath the assets. Check lighting and shadows before placing assets together.

## Delivery rules

Keep raw generations separate from processed production assets. Do not bake readable UI text, resource values, selection outlines or ownership colors into paintings. Confirm transparency rather than accepting a painted checkerboard. Preserve provenance and license information for any non-generated source materials. Generated sheets need cropping, consistency checks and in-game size review before integration.
