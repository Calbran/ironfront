# Farmland materials

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Asset: `apps/web/public/art/farmland/materials-v1.png` (1254 × 1254), four equal material quadrants: green crops, wheat, plowed soil, pasture. The renderer clips these to seeded adjoining field parcels and orients crop rows with parcel boundaries. The reference photographs supplied by the user informed the agricultural patchwork direction; no photographic imagery was copied into the map.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production terrain material atlas for Ironfront, a muted naturalistic aerial strategy map. Create one square 2048x2048 raster atlas divided into exactly FOUR equal square quadrants in a 2 by 2 grid with NO padding, NO gutters, NO border, NO text. Each quadrant is an uninterrupted full-bleed farm-field surface seen from precisely vertical orthographic overhead, consistent fine physical scale and soft diffuse lighting, no perspective, no horizon. Upper left: subdued olive-green growing crops in fine parallel vertical rows, subtle organic foliage and soil between rows. Upper right: muted ochre golden wheat stubble in parallel vertical harvest rows, dusty rather than bright yellow. Lower left: brown cultivated earth with realistic fine parallel vertical plow furrows, subtle dry soil clods. Lower right: sage green pasture meadow, fine grass texture and faint parallel mowing strips. Texture detail should be delicate and readable at multiple zoom levels, not noisy. All quadrants equally lit, no directional shadows or vignettes, natural small variations but no big unique landmarks. Do NOT paint multiple field parcels within a quadrant; each quadrant is one continuous material to be clipped to separate polygonal fields by the game. No houses, roads, trees, machinery, water, labels, shadows, or decorative framing. The atlas will create varied adjoining agricultural parcels in game. Restrained earthy palette compatible with dark teal ocean and desaturated green terrain.

## Regional land use

The town-centered blocks are superseded by a seeded shared-vertex parcel lattice across selected plains regions. Cultivated fields are inset from shared boundaries to form verges; regular rural lanes and scattered ordinary-scale trees establish hedged countryside. Placement is independent of settlement presence, with road, river, town and terrain clearances. Existing settlement locations are preserved. Field textures retain their world scale as the agricultural footprint grows.

## Boundary-filling fields

Agricultural regions now clip the parcel lattice to their complete land contours rather than rejecting parcels that cross a boundary. Concave outlines and inland-water holes are triangulated; roads, rivers, settlement clearings and mountain obstacles are subtracted from each field. Edge pieces retain the same world-space crop texture and orientation. Narrow parcel verges remain. Farmland is also masked to the smoothed display coastline to prevent raster/display-edge leakage. This supersedes the isolated-parcel rejection rule for edge fields: partial edge parcels fill the remaining region footprint. Scenery clearance consumes actual clipped fragments.
