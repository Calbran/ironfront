# Angled streets and irregular parcels

Implemented a separate diorama comparison with a diagonal boulevard, two seeded cross streets and six convex parcels. Full building envelope containment, road/lot separation and front-door path clearance determine placement. Existing architectural scale is preserved. Polygon paving and inset garden shapes replace rectangular slabs in this study; the civic neighborhood is connected through its rear-market street.

Validation includes five deterministic seeds, triangular parcels, outward clear entrances and exact fitted-count accounting. Browser coverage exercises mode switching, seed regeneration, day/dusk/winter, phone width and return to the regular budget controls. Screenshots are under .impeccable/review/angled-district. Full suite, typecheck and build results are recorded in the task response. Layout remains an authored flat street framework; this is not generated-world rollout.
