# Courtyard clearance and rooftop fit

User feedback identified crowded rear windows, unused paving and a flickering brass rooftop patch. Courtyard wings now use shallower models with unchanged facade width and floor height. Planted courts, benches and gardens occupy the reserved court and unused frontage slots. A generation invariant checks clearance around the court.

The brass patch was a water-tank lid sharing the tank's top plane; the tank also intersected the pitched roof. Raised the tank onto iron supports and a platform, with distinct lid and tank surfaces.

Validation: typecheck, all 32 test files and production build passed. Final browser checks passed with no errors, including model envelopes, detail tiers, seed regeneration and dusk/winter phone views. Reviewed a street-view capture. The geometry overlap is removed; a still capture alone does not establish temporal flicker behavior at every camera position. Changes remain confined to the experimental city study and its generation helpers.
