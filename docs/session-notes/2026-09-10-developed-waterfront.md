# Developed channel and arched bridges

User requested a fuller water channel, environmental accents and a shallow masonry bridge arch. Raised study channel water by 1.25 miniature units. Quay walls reuse brickwork and receive stone caps. Dedicated continuous subdivided bridge meshes carry a shallow arch through deck, paved surface and capped parapets, replacing the former segmented flat crossing. A small open wooden rowboat sits at the waterline with benches, an oar and a sloped mooring rope to a bollard. Sparse cargo accents remain on the promenade.

Added Waterfront camera preset and close capture to river browser validation. Typecheck/build and browser verification cover day, seed variation, winter/dusk and phone mode switching. Screenshots live under .impeccable/review/river-district. Changes are presentation-only; boat motion, traffic and collision semantics are not implemented.
