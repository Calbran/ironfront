# Review corridor

First vertical slice in the physical pacing view: one representative city, a compact farmstead within a large agricultural district, and a compact settlement/redoubt. Searches bounded nearby combinations and orthogonal route alternatives, samples the road's land footprint, rejects river intersections and unrelated POI/city envelopes, and declines if none pass. No bridges are invented. Road endpoints join city center streets and east/west country-kit sockets; buildings and roads are never scaled up.

Road width is five model units. Large crop rectangles intersecting the buffered road are omitted rather than painted underneath. Sparse tree instances dress the roadside. A gold screen-space-width guide appears when zoomed out, separate from physical road width. The review has whole-corridor and three stop-focus buttons and a constant infantry walking-time calculation. Full tactical city substitution, terrain-grade adaptation, polygon-level clearance proof, curved road junctions, authoritative path orders and economy integration remain future work. Tree dressing is visual, not tactical cover.

Validation: three corridor tests cover determinism, entrance sockets, width, no forced river crossings, crop clearance and Meridian generation. Typecheck/build pass (existing bundle-size warning).
